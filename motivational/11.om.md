I’d rate your original thought **8.5/10**. The core idea is strong: **don’t wait until a difficult task feels easy; start with small efforts until your brain becomes comfortable doing it.**

I’d improve the wording and make it more powerful:

> **No matter how difficult a task seems, tell yourself: “It is possible, and I can start small.” Keep making small efforts consistently until the difficult task becomes familiar—and eventually, effortless.**

Or a shorter, punchier version:

> **Don’t fear the difficulty. Start small, keep going, and repeat until what once felt difficult becomes effortless.**

### Your underlying principle is excellent

**Difficult → Small effort → Repetition → Familiarity → Confidence → Effortless**

The key is not actually convincing yourself that _every difficult task is easy_. It's better to tell yourself:

**“It may be difficult, but I don't need to finish it right now. I only need to make the next small effort.”**

That makes your thought more realistic and sustainable.
