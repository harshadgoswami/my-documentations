Your core idea is **very strong — 8.5/10**. The message is about taking responsibility for both **thoughts and actions**, but “each move” and “write your future” can be made more natural and memorable.

My favorite version:

**Every thought shapes your mind.
Every move shapes your destiny.
Every choice writes your future.**

So choose your thoughts wisely,
move with purpose,
and build the future you want.

### 🔥 More powerful and concise

**Every thought matters.
Every move matters.
Every choice matters.**

Because your thoughts shape your actions,
your actions shape your habits,
and your habits shape your destiny.

### 💭 If you want it as a personal daily reminder

**My future is not decided by luck.
It is being written every day—
by my thoughts,
by my choices,
and by every move I make.**

**My pick: the third one.** It feels less like a motivational quote and more like a personal commitment—which makes it stronger when you read it every day.
